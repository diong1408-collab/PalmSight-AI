# PalmSight-AI - Satellite-Powered Palm Oil Supply and Risk Intelligence
It is an Earth Observation and AI that turns satellite and environmental signals into actionable palm oil supply-risk intelligence for investors and businesses.

Problem: Palm oil supply can be affected by vegetation stress, extreme rainfall, flooding, drought, and land use change. Conventional financial information may arrive after real world changes have already occurred.

AI Approach:
1. Process environmental observations.
2. Calculate changes and trends over time.
3. Detect unusual changes using statistical or machine-learning method.
4. Combine indicators into an explainable risk score.
5. Generate a concise AI-assisted explaination.
